Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At HOW TO SELL ACADEMY, we are committed to providing high-quality training and educational programs. Please read this Refund Policy carefully before making payment.

Course Enrolment

Once payment has been successfully made and your enrollment has been confirmed, your space is reserved for you in the selected program.

Refund Eligibility

Due to the digital nature of our courses, coaching sessions, downloadable materials, and online community access, payments are generally non-refundable.

However, a refund may be considered if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same enrollment.
  • You were charged successfully but did not receive access due to a verified technical issue that could not be resolved.
  • A program is cancelled by HOW TO SELL ACADEMY without a suitable alternative being provided.

Requesting a Refund

If you believe you qualify for a refund, please contact us within 7 days of your payment and provide your payment details and reason for the request. Each request will be reviewed individually.

Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on your payment provider.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Refund Policy, please contact HOW TO SELL ACADEMY using the contact information provided on this website.
